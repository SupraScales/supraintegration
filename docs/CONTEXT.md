# Supra Integration Business Context

This document records stable business context for future work. Use it to make decisions without requiring the same background to be restated each session.

## Business

- Supra Integration is an AI automation agency. We audit a business's systems, find where revenue leaks, and deploy AI agents into the gaps. We replace workflows and headcount; we do not sell software people have to learn.
- The sister brand is SupraScales ([suprascales.com](https://suprascales.com)), a TikTok Shop growth agency for US DTC brands. It uses the same visual system but offers a different service.
- The parent entity is Vryvo Vault LLC, based in Salt Lake City, Utah.
- Owner: Brayden Olson.
- Co-founder: Damon Norton.

## Who We Sell To

- US-only small businesses, typically with roughly 4–8 employees and at least $10,000 in monthly revenue.
- Strongest verticals: roofing, HVAC, home services, med spas, and detailing.
- The buyer is an owner-operator, not a technical person. They are losing money to missed calls, slow lead response, manual quoting, and follow-ups nobody sends.
- A typical engagement is approximately $4,000 for setup plus approximately $500 per month.

## The Site's Job

- The site has exactly one job: book a call. Every design decision must serve that goal. It is not a portfolio and not an art piece.
- Most traffic arrives from Instagram on phones. Mobile performance is a revenue issue, not merely a technical one. A slow page costs real leads.

## Voice

- Be direct, simple, and concrete. Say what the thing does.
- Banned words: "AI-powered," "cutting-edge," "revolutionize," "unlock," "seamless," "leverage," and "empower."
- Never promise guaranteed results.
- Never invent client names, numbers, or testimonials. If a number is needed and none is available, ask the user.
- Say "proprietary software," not "AI-powered platform."

## Known Gaps (Do Not Fix Without Asking)

- The CTA is currently a `mailto:` link. There is no contact form. This is a known conversion leak.
